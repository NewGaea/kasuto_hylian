# Oh, Holy Night (Holiday)

This is translation produced in 2024. This is a translation exercise for the sake of community building, and the translation makes great efforts to fit the world of Hyrule in which there is no established "Jesus Christ" nor is there the same concept of sin as found in Christian tradition. The lyrics are not merely translated, but localized into a form that would fit with our approach to Neo-Modern Hylian.

## Verse A

This is the most common and universal verse in the Hylian equivalent of *Oh, Holy Night*, and this verse is often performed by re-enactors after the Hero figure draws or receives their Sacred Sword.

### Oh, Holy Night, the Stars are Shining Brightly, It is the night of our dear saviour's birth.

```ngloss
\ex Naistri alba, toërensu zelde folkas, naistre tairas ab bunshasu añothe
\ft On this holy night, the world's fate is turning, 'tis the night of The Savior's birth
```

### Long lay the world in sin and error pining, 'til he appeared and the soul felt its worth.

```ngloss
\ex Toërën mabarthag ab ënt makëume, jade ëru chepoes az kishoku.
\ft Too long the worlds have steeped in mortal danger, until he drew the Great Fabled Sword forth.
```

### The thrill of hope, the weary world rejoices for yonder breaks a new and glorious morn(ing).

```ngloss
\ex Xipi dama! Vosu alazas ab upën, ëru krozons ab malëue sékomag
\ft With new-born hope we raise up our prayers, He must fell the malice now spreading.
```

### Fall on your knees

```ngloss
\ex Vosu wanhën,
\ft Your voices,
```

### Oh! hear the angel voices!

```ngloss
\ex namën, chepayën, maludons!
\ft your hands, your hearts: sing loudly!
```

### Oh! Night divine!

```ngloss
\ex Zanaistre!
\ft Tonight!
```

### Oh! Night when Christ was born.

```ngloss
\ex Upons ab Kishoku ënk Hezhu!
\ft Pray! For Sword and Hero!
```

### Oh! Night divine!

"Tonight! Sing out! Tonight! May we be saved!"

```ngloss
\ex Zanaistre!
\ft Tonight!
```

### Oh! Night…

```ngloss
\ex Ludans!
\ft Sing out!
```

### Oh! Night divine!

```ngloss
\ex Bunshans ab nosu!
\ft You must save us!
```
