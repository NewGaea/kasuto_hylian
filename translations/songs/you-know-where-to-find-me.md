# You Know Where to Find Me

This presents an updated translation of Matthew West's *You Know Where To Find Me* inspired by the original partial translation  posted by [QBri](contributors/qbri.md) on [the Vinculum](archival/vinculum/kantën-ont-hilane/01-qbri.md).

## Taiu Zavokas ab Dono Trovag Bagu

The first change we make is to scrap QBri's proposed accusative form in favor of the canonical first person pronoun in the title. Neither Modern nor Neo-Modern Hylian distinguishes nominative from accusative, there are only nouns marked for role by the particles *ab* for the direct object (accusative) and *ank* for the indirect. A lack of particle is used for the nominative.
