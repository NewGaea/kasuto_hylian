# Hirlërde daia Hilañve - Chapter 2: Hylian Verbs I – Simple Tense

Now that we know how to build sentences, we should try to understand the elements of each sentence. The most important class of word for this purpose is the verb, which is the answer to question: “What is happening in this sentence?”

Luckily for learners of Modern Hylian, verbs are completely regular, and none of them break the paradigm.

## Non-Finite Verbs

Non-finite verbs are verbs which have no duration or specific place in time. They fill the roles of infinitives, gerunds, and participles, and at their most basic always end with the suffix _-ag_.

This means that they can express habitual actions, like:

---
```ngloss
\ex Lïnkera promag.  
\ft Link walks (regularly).
```
---

The non-finite form of verbs is their dictionary form, and can be translated as “to _verb_, _verbing_, _verb-_s, _verb-_ed” and is used in many cases where a verb is relative to another verb in the sentence or wider context.

## Simple Tenses

Hylian verbs have tense, aspect, and mood. This lesson is only focused on the basic tense structure, and we will address the other two elements later on.

| **Non-Finite** | **Present** | **Past** | **Future** |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- | ---------- |
| -ag            | -as         | -es      | -os        |
| _zhatag_       | _zhatas_    | _zhates_ | _zhatos_   |
| to say, saying | say, says   | said     | Will say   |

The tenses of Modern Hylian are _present_, _past_, and _future_. This means that they do not require a helper word or phrase to express things that will happen in the future, unlike English.

## Agreement?

Modern Hylian verbs do not need to agree with any other element of a sentence. Where English has, for example, “I say, you say, he/she/it says,” Hylian uses the exact same form: “I _zhatas_, you _zhatas_, he/she/it _zhatas_.” This makes the verb structure easier to learn, as all you need to know is whether the verb is in its non-finite conjugation or if it has a tense applied.

## Samples

The following samples help to demonstrate how to conjugate verbs:

### Past Tense
---
```ngloss
\ex Lïnkera koemes ab koemën mokan.
\ft Link ate a lot of food.
```
---
### Present Tense
---
```ngloss
\ex Lïnkera lërdas ab raz lërde ma.
\ft “Link is reading a big book._”
```
---
### Future Tense
---
```ngloss
\ex Lïnkera zhatos ab boke.
\ft “Link will say nothing.
```
