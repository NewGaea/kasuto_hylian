# Hirlërde daia Hilañve - Unit 1 Dictionary

Thank you for your interest in learning Neo-Modern Hylian. We hope you've been reading all of the lessons so far, and picked up a bit about the words we've shared. This is a bonus for the Unit 1 compilation, with more complete definitions for all of the words we've covered in the first 5 lessons. For simplicity, they're listed in English alphabetical order -- since we haven't explained the Hylian alphabet yet.

## Word List

+ **ab** (_particle_) 1. prepositional marker of the direct object
+ **akime** (_noun_) 1. friend, ally, comrade
+ **amkane** (_noun_) 1. love, affection
+ **ank** (_particle_) 1. prepositional marker of indirect objects
+ **bënkata** (_adjective_) 1. welcome, appreciated, desired or desirable 2. of introduction, introductory, welcoming
+ **bënkati** (_topical noun_) 1. welcome, greetings, well met; as _bënkati_, this word may be a whole greeting of its own at any time of day or night
+ **biua** (_adjective_) 1. brown, earthy color
+ **boke** (_noun_) 1. nothing, void, nothingness
+ **daia** (_conjunction_) 1. of, from, about
+ **diarag** (_verb_) 1. to give 2. to grant a wish, to make something real
+ **diare** (_noun_) 1. gift 2. blessing, boon; a gift from someone of higher station
+ **grohën** (_noun_) 1. cereal grains such as oats, barley, corn, rice (uncountable)
+ **hilañve** (_noun_) 1. (any) Hylian language
+ **hirlërde** (_noun_) 1. primer, basic textbook
+ **hiose** (_noun_) 1. child, offspring; _hiose_ implies a familial relationship between some _piante_ and the _hiose_, contrast with _kime_
+ **hira** (_adjective_) 1. one, sole, only, unique
+ **hire** (_noun_) 1. one, only one; this generally refers to concept of "one" rather than a way to describe one _of_ something
+ **hite** (_noun_) 1. person; may refer to any sentient or sapient being, as perceived by the speaker, this is not equivalent to words derived from Latin _homo_ "human, mankind," but closer to the English _person_ or the Japanese 人 (_hito_) "person"
+ **kvïcane** (_noun_) 1. fox; formal word, also preferred for use by those who view foxes with respect or as spiritual beings
+ **kime** (_noun_) 1. child, young person; this does not imply a familial relationship, only that the referent is young, and usually has not reached puberty, though _kime_ can refer to anybody who is not an adult
+ **koemag** (_verb_) 1. to eat, to consume food
+ **koeme** (_noun_) 1. food 2. (generic) meal, snack; this generally refers to an instance of eating, rather than the concept of a meal
+ **lërdag** (_verb_) 1. to read, to learn by reading
+ **lërde** (_noun_) 1. book, written record 2. letter, written message, missive
+ **Lïnkera** (_proper noun_) 1. Link, a name commonly attributed to legendary heroes who are connected with the Cycle of Creation and Demise, particularly that subset also called _The Legends of Zelda_
+ **ma** (_adjective_) 1. big, large, grand, thick
+ **malsëte** (_noun_) 1. meal; a socially-defined style of eating based on time, company, or both 2. meal, course, dish; a generally prepared collection of complementary foods prepared and served by a cook or chef
+ **moka** (_adjective_) 1. much, many, a lot 2. more-or-less, mostly
+ **pastya** (_adjective_) 1. quick, fast, rapid, speedy
+ **piante** (_noun_) 1. parent; _piante_ implies a familial relationship between the _piante_ and some _hiose_
+ **promag** (_verb_) 1. to walk, to stroll, to move about on foot
+ **rausën** (_noun_) 1. rice (uncountable)
+ **raz** (_article_) 1. a, an; the indefinite article refers to an unclarified thing, something the speaker _may_ know, but which the listener is not expected to know the identity of at this point
+ **sihia** (_adjective_) 1. green, grue, teal; generally that range of colors that is equidistant or closer to blue than to yellow on a color wheel
+ **simia** (_adjective_) 1. purple, violet, magenta; generally that range of colors which is equidistant or closer to blue than to red on a color wheel
+ **soke** (_noun_) 1. shirt, tunic, blouse; a garment worn to cover the torso from shoulders to waist or lower, but generally no longer than the knees
+ **tairag** (_verb_) 1. to be, to exist
+ **taz** (_article_) 1. the; the definite article refers to something the speaker trusts their listener to be able to identify, usually something which has been established earlier in conversation
+ **tiudre** (_noun_) 1. day, daytime, midday, afternoon; this generally refers to the period of a day from which the sun reaches its apex in the sky until the colors of the sky begin to change
+ **toboke** (_noun_) 1. dress, gown, robe; a garment which covers the body from the shoulders to the knees or longer, there is some overlap between _toboke_ and _soke_ for garments which are close to the knees
+ **vate** (_noun_) 1. egg, ovum 2. pregnancy
+ **vimia** (_noun_) 1. pink, rosy
+ **vina** (_adjective_) 1. good, well; of positive quality
+ **wole** (_noun_) 1. wolf, fox; wild predators of the Canidae family and monsters of similar form, formally refers only to wolves and _wolfos_, but in many regions predatory and scavenging foxes are included in this scope
+ **Zëldase** (_proper noun_) 1. Zelda, a name traditionally given to the firstborn daughters of the Hyrulean royal family, and closely associated with the Cycle of Creation and Destruction, particularly through those epics also called _The Legends of Zelda_
+ **zhatag** (_verb_) 1. to say, to speak 2. to recite, to read aloud
