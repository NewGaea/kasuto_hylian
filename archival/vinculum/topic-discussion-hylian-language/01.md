# This topic is for discussion related to the Hylian Language.

## by [[Kasuto of Kataan](contributors/kasuto.md)], Thu, 2003-Jul-31 @ 19:12

This topic is for discussion related to the Hylian Language. It can be about the language itself, like discussions about the linguistics or grammar. If you need help on a specific aspect of the language, post your questions here. If you want a translation, or have a question about a certain grammar rule, go ahead and ask. You can also suggest new ideas to add to the language, like words or grammar. Although this subject is intended to discuss the Hylian Language, you can also discuss things about other foreign languages as well. As a person who loves studying foreign languages, I have nothing against talking about languages other than Hylian.
