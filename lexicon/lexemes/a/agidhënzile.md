# *agidhënzile*

# Neo-Modern Hylian

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

From *agitha dënzile* "butterfly wand," referring to the fact that they are safe for even children to handle with minimal safety precautions.

## Pronunciation

+ **IPA Broad**: /a.*,gi*.ðen.**'zi**.le/

## 1. Noun

### agidhënzile (pl. agidhënzilën)

1. handheld firework
2. butterfly wand
