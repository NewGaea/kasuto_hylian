# akte

# Neo-Modern Hylian
## Alternative Forms

## Etymology
### External Origin
From [**akt**](lexicon/lexemes/a/akt) "to the", a contraction of [**ak**](lexicon/lexemes/a/ak) "to, toward(s)" and [**taz**](lexicon/lexemes/t/taz), analysed and read as a word stem meaning "move to."

## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**: /'ak.te/

## Noun

### akte (plural *aktën*)
1. a, ah
	1. This is the name of the first letter in the Hylian [*aktaite*](lexicon/lexemes/a/aktaite.md), romanized as \<a> and pronounced as /a/
2. movement to, movement towards
