## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**: /'al.be/

## Noun

### albe (plural *albën*)
1. divinity, holiness
2. blessing
3. purity
4. purification ritual
5. Albe, Sacred Month
	1. The Hylian month equivalent to July; this name represents the blessings of the Goddesses in both midsummer and the middle of the year
