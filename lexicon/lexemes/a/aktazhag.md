# aktazhag

# Neo-Modern Hylian
## Alternative Forms

## Etymology
### External Origin
This word was coined by [Austin](contributors/austin-springer.md) based on Kasuto's expression *aktazhá*, a vulgar expression equivalent to "damn it to hell."
### Internal Origin
From [**akte**](lexicon/lexemes/a/akte) "motion towards" and [**tazho**](lexicon/lexemes/t/tazho) "down, below, under".

## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**:
	- **aktazhag**: /ak.'ta.Zag/

## Verb

### aktazhag
1. (spiritual) to curse, to condemn
2. (legal) to condemn, to judge, to sentence
3. to fall, to become prone
4. to expose, to become vulnerable
