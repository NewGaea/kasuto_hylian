## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**: /a.'la.zaːg/

## Verb

### alazaag (present *alazaas*, past *alazaes*, future *alazaos*)
1. to behave kindly, to offer friendship
	1. to behave in a good manner to others, especially those who live near oneself
2. to act generously
