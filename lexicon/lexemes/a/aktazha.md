# aktazha

# Neo-Modern Hylian
## Alternative Forms
- **aktazhá** (interjection, vulgar)

## Etymology
### External Origin
This word was coined by [Austin](contributors/austin-springer.md) based on Kasuto's expression *aktazhá*, a vulgar expression equivalent to "damn it to hell."
### Internal Origin
From [**akte**](lexicon/lexemes/a/akte) "motion towards" and [**tazho**](lexicon/lexemes/t/tazho) "down, below, under".

## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**:
	- **aktazha**: /ak.'ta.Za/
	- **aktazhá**: /,ak.ta.'Zae/

## Adjective

### aktazha (plural *aktazhan*)
1. (spiritual) cursed, condemned
2. (legal) condemned, judged, sentenced
3. fallen, prone
4. exposed, vulnerable

## Interjection

### aktazhá
1. (independent) damn!, damn it! to hell with this!
	1. presented in [Kasuto's common expressions](archival/kasuto_hli/09-arkthën.md), literally meant to translate as "to hell" (presumed: [*ak*](lexicon/lexemes/a/ak) [*tazhá*](lexicon/lexemes/t/tazhe)) equivalent to Modern English "Damn it to hell!"
2. (descriptive) cursed, damned, damnable
