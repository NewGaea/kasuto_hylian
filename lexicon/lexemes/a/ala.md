## Adjective

### ala (plural *alan*)
1. there, that place
2. then, that time

It is important to note that this can only be used as an adjective, and must be inflected as adverbial [**alok**](lexicon/lexemes/a/alok) or pronominal [**alu**](lexicon/lexemes/a/alu) to be used in other parts of a sentence.
