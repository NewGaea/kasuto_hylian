# aktazhag

# Neo-Modern Hylian
## Alternative Forms

## Etymology
### External Origin
This word was coined by [Austin](contributors/austin-springer.md) based on Kasuto's expression *aktazhá*, a vulgar expression equivalent to "damn it to hell."
### Internal Origin
From [**akte**](lexicon/lexemes/a/akte) "motion towards" and [**tazho**](lexicon/lexemes/t/tazho) "down, below, under".

## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**:
	- **aktazhe: /ak.'ta.Ze/

## Noun

### aktazhe
1. curse, hex, karma, karmic weight, malice
2. (legal) condemnation, judgement, sentence
3. fall, proneness
4. exposure, vulnerability
