# aktaita

# Neo-Modern Hylian
## Alternative Forms
- **akta•ia**: (archaic, Kasutan form) original romanized spelling by Kasuto

## Etymology
From [**akte**](lexicon/lexemes/a/akte) and [**aite**](lexicon/lexemes/ai/aite), the first two letters of the Modern Hylian script in formal order.

## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**: /akt.'ai.te/

## Adjective

### aktaita (plural *aktaitan*)
1. alphabetic, alphabetical
