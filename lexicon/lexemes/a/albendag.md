# albendag

# Neo-Modern Hylian
## Alternative Forms

## Etymology
Uncertain. Possibly related to [**albode**](lexicon/lexemes/a/albode.md) or Modern English [abode](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/abode#English) "place of residence."

## Pronunciation
- **IPA Broad**: /al.'bEn.dag/

## Verb

### albendag (present *albendas*, past *albendes*, future *albendos*)
1. to host, to welcome guests into one's home 
2. to provide comfortable shelter
3. to comfort
4. to keep safe, to protect
