## Verb

### tetrag

1. to wander, to sojourn, to travel
	1. to move about the lands unattached to a fixed home, point of origin, or point of destination
2. to exile, to banish
	1. to be stripped of citizenship and the right to live in some particular place, often as the result of some misdeed
3. to live lawlessly, to live outside the law
	1. to be stripped of the protections and benefits of the law, usually as a punishment for some great criminal offense in place of execution
