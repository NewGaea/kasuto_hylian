# _tetra_

# Neo-Modern Hylian

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### External Etymology

From _Tetra_, the name of the pirate captain who is secretly Princess Zelda in _The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker._ While this name is likely from the Greek _tetra_ "four," we've interpreted the meaning based on the role of the name Tetra within _The Wind Waker_'s story.

Coined by [Austin Springer](contributors/austin-springer.md) via [Nintendo](contributors/nintendo.md) on 11 August 2025.

## Pronunciation

+ **IPA Broad**: /**'te**.tɹa/

## Adjective

### _tetra_ (plural _tetran_)

1. (a) wandering, journeying, traveling, (b) moving about, ghostly, phantom
	1. to travel broadly, especially without a fixed home to return to
	2. moving about without aim, purpose, or a predictable course
	3. (of a place or object) moving about despite lacking apparent means of doing so
2. exiled, outcast
	1. unwelcome in society
	2. driven out from society
3. criminal, rogue, renegade
	1. prone to abnormal or irregular behaviour, especially violent behaviour, but also including anti-social behaviours.
	2. possessed of often-uncontrollable impulses or urges
