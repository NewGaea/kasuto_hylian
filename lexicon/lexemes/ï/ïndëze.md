# _ïndëze_

# Neo-Modern Hylian

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

### External Etymology

From Reddit's [Telephone game 698](https://www.reddit.com/r/conlangs/comments/1mewcfl/comment/n6hro4i/), from Tokén _eñdzẹ_ / eɲ.d͡zɤ / "fish, tadpole," which is in turn from Vu _ndzɛ́_ / ⁿd͡ʒɛ˦˧˨ / "fish egg, roe."

### Internal Etymology

From Zoran \*_ʕïndëzh_ / ʕɪn˨.dɛʒ˦ / "egg, roe." This is the first word coined for our Zoran language.

## Pronunciation

+ **IPA Broad**: /ɪn.ˈdɛ.ze/

## 1. Noun

### ïndêze (_pl._ ïndêzën)

1. Aquatic egg, fish egg, frog egg, roe
2. Zora egg, Zora hatchling
