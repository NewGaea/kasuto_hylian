# _magza_

# Neo-Modern Hylian

## Alternative Forms

**Plural**: _magzan_

## Etymology

### External Etymology

Possibly from English _mwah_, an onomatopoeia used for the sound of kissing or smooching.

## Pronunciation

+ **IPA Broad**: /**'mag**.zag/

## 1. Adjective

### magza (pl. magzan)

1. kissable, smoochable
2. delicious, tasty
3. cute, adorable
