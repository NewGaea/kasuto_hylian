## 1. Verb

### magzag (pres _magzas_, past _magzes_, future magzos)

1. to kiss, to smooch
	1. to press one's lips against something
	2. to smack one's lips together noisily
2. to taste, to sample the flavor of, to taste test
	1. to use one's tongue to assess the flavor of food or drink, especially during preparation
	2. to use something that is not yet finished for assessment of its qualities or progress
