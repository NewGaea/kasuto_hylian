# _magzag tabantho_

# Neo-Modern Hylian

## Alternative Forms

## Etymology

A compound of _Tabantha_, a frontier region in north-western Hyrule, and _magzag_ "to kiss" to describe the unseemly and passionate manner of kissing performed by those far from the strictures of the Castle Town and Nobility.

## Pronunciation

+ **IPA Broad**: /**'mag**.zag ta.**'ban**.tho/

## 1. Verb Phrase

### magzag tabantho (pl. inflected-lexeme)

1. to kiss like a Tabanthan, to French kiss, to make out, to swap spit
	1. to kiss mouth to mouth, with an open mouth and the use of tongue
