# The Hylian Linguistic Society's Compiled Lexicon

This lexicon is a list of words only, compiled in English alphabetical order. There is a **dictionary** available for more detailed definitions of words, and the old lexicon files are still in place for now.

For details about how entries are listed in this new format, please check [[lexicon-guidelines|our Lexicon Guidelines]] page.

As of this update (beginning in July 2023), all words from [Kasuto's HLI](http://kasuto.net/language.php?main=language/language.html), our archived Vinculum threads, [[Hylian Community Wordlist]] and [[Ont Hilana (Interlinear of Thread).md|Ont Hilana]], and our own creations are afforded first-class status as documented Hylian words under the Hylian Language Society.

This lexicon uses IPA to show the pronunciation of words.

**NOTE:** While \<_jh_\> and \<_zh_\> are both valid romanizations, this lexicon listing prefers \<_zh_\> to parallel the \<_sh_\> structure.

## A - Á

+ **Armose** \[] _creature, monster._ 1. Armos, walking statue 1. a class of monsters carved from stone and imbued with an animating force, they awaken to chase trespassers who come near (or touch) them 1. from Armos (_The Legend of Zelda, Link to the Past, etc._); from _arme_ "statue, sculpture" + _fose_ "fear, monster"

## AU

+ **Auruno** _given, masculine_ 1. _undefined_ 1. believed to be related to the name _Rauruno_ "Rauru", this name is believed to have special significance to the Knights of Hyrule during the era of the Hero of Time. 1. from Auru (_Skyward Sword_)

## B

+ **Bibidonto** \[bi.'bi.don.to] _given, masculine._ 1. Beedle, Beetle; Protector 1. the name of a mysterious traveling merchant; in the modern age he can often be found near stables with a beetle-shaped pack full of his wares 2. from Beedle (_Wind Waker, Skyward Sword, etc._), from _bibidoe_ "beetle"
+ **Bokoblïne** (bokoblïne) _creature, monster._ 1. Bokoblin 1. small and lithe variety of goblins which live in all sorts of environments; they are often pressed into serving bulblins or moblins 1. from Bokoblin (_Wind Waker, Twilight Princess, etc._)
+ **Bulblïne** (bulblïne) _noun._ 1. Bulblin 1. a tribe of goblins skilled in taming and riding _bulboën_ "giant boars," they primarily live a nomadic life on the open plains of Hyrule 1. from Bulblin (_Twilight Princess_)
