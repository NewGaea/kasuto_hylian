# _abaz_

# Neo-Modern Hylian

## Alternative Forms

+ _ab taz_: formal, full form
+ _abz_: shorter variant
+ _az_: shorter variant

## Etymology

A contraction of the words _ab taz_, can take several forms.

## Pronunciation

+ **IPA Broad**
	+ **abaz**: /**'a**.baz/
	+ **abz**: /abz/
	+ **az**: /az/

## Particle Compound

### abaz, abz, az

1. direct object particle combined with definite article
	1. the
		1. identifies a noun as an established part of discourse or a piece of expected knowledge, and serving as direct object
