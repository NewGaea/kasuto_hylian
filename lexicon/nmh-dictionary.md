# Dictionary of Neo-Modern Hylian<div class="subtitle">As compiled by Ozreth and Jerin of the Hylian Linguistic Society</div>

This dictionary is a list of words, compiled in Hylian ***aktaita*** ordering, including links to their individual word definitions. Unlike the [Hylian Lexicon](lexicon/hylian_lexicon_en_2023.md), which is an English-sorted list of words, this dictionary does not contain any Hylian text.

This Dictionary project follows up from the Lexicon mentioned above, but aims to be more concise and direct, allowing the word pages themselves to fill in greater details. The only information presented on this page is the word, what part of speech it is, and its primary glosses in English.

## A - Á

1. [**-a**](lexicon/lexemes/a/-a.md): 1. (*grammatical ending*) -ish, singular adjective suffix
2. [**-an**](lexicon/lexemes/a/-an.md): 1. (*grammatical ending*) -ishes, plural adjective suffix
3. [**ab**](lexicon/lexemes/a/ab.md): 1. (*particle*) direct object marker
